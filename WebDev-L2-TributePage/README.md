# WebDev-L2-TributePage — Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam Tribute

> **OIBSIP Track**: Web Development & Designing  
> **Level**: 2 | **Task**: 2  
> **Tech Stack**: HTML5, CSS3 (Dual-typography, dual-background architecture)

---

## 📌 Project Overview
A visually engaging tribute page dedicated to Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam ("The Missile Man of India" and 11th President of India). Engineered with content-first semantic markup, exploring serif vs. sans-serif typography, milestone timeline components, and styled quote cards.

---

## 🎯 Acceptance Criteria & Checklist
- [x] **Title & Tagline**: Immediately identifies Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam and his historical timeline (1931-2015).
- [x] **Prominent Portrait Media**: Clean SVG vector portrait of Dr. Kalam with caption.
- [x] **Original Biographical Narrative**: 4 structured paragraphs covering early life in Rameswaram, ISRO SLV-III launch, missile development programs, and presidency.
- [x] **Milestone Timeline**: Scannable timeline covering 1931, 1969, 1982, 1997 (Bharat Ratna), 2002 (Presidency), and 2015 (Legacy).
- [x] **Notable Quote Block**: Visually distinct quotation styled in serif typography (`Lora`) with citation to *Wings of Fire*.
- [x] **Dual Background Architecture**: High-contrast dark navy hero (`#0f172a`) transitioning to warm reading paper (`#fcfbf9`).
- [x] **Dual Font Styles**: Exploring serif headings & quotes alongside modern sans-serif body text.
- [x] **Citations & References**: Direct external links to historical resources.

---

Open `WebDev-L2-TributePage/index.html` directly in any browser.
