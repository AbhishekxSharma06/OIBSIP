Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

1931 – 2015 • The Missile Man of India & The People's President

Biography & Legacy

A Life Dedicated to Science, Youth, and the Nation

Born on October 15, 1931, in the coastal pilgrimage town of Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, Dr. Kalam came from humble beginnings. To support his family's household expenses, he distributed newspapers as a boy. Driven by an insatiable curiosity for flight and aerodynamics, he went on to earn a degree in aeronautical engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology.

Dr. Kalam spent over four decades in public research, primarily at the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) and the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO). As Project Director, he spearheaded the development of India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle (SLV-III), which successfully placed the Rohini satellite into low Earth orbit in 1980.

Widely revered as the "Missile Man of India," he played a seminal role in architecting the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme (IGMDP), resulting in strategic defence assets including Agni and Prithvi. In 1998, he served as Chief Scientific Adviser during the Pokhran-II nuclear tests, cementing the country's national security foundation.

Serving as the 11th President of India from 2002 to 2007, Kalam transformed the presidency into a vibrant institution accessible to ordinary citizens. Affectionately named "The People's President," he spent his post-presidential years visiting universities, engaging with over five million young students, and advocating passionately for education, technological self-reliance, and moral integrity.

“Dream, dream, dream. Dreams transform into thoughts, and thoughts result in action. You have to dream before your dreams can come true.”

— Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam, Wings of Fire: An Autobiography
Milestones

Key Chronology & Achievements

1931

Early Life in Rameswaram

Born to Jainulabdeen and Ashiamma in Rameswaram; demonstrated perseverance and dedication to academic excellence despite economic hardship.

1969

ISRO & SLV-III Project Directorship

Joined the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) and led India's first indigenous satellite launch vehicle project to landmark success.

1982

Architect of the IGMDP

Directed the Integrated Guided Missile Development Programme, engineering key indigenous defense systems (Agni, Prithvi, Akash, Trishul).

1997

Bharat Ratna Conferred

Awarded India's highest civilian honor, the Bharat Ratna, for his invaluable contributions to scientific research and aerospace modernization.

2002

11th President of India

Elected as President with overwhelming cross-party support; championed 'India Vision 2020' to transform the nation into a developed society.

2015

Eternal Legacy in Education

Passed away on July 27, 2015, while delivering a lecture to students at IIM Shillong, departing the world doing what he loved most: teaching.
